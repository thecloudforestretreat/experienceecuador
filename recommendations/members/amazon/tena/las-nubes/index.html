Why stay at Las Nubes in Tena, Ecuador

Las Nubes is a cozy lodging option for travelers who want to experience the biodiversity of Ecuador while enjoying a quiet and comfortable base in nature. The setting works especially well for birdwatchers, nature lovers, and travelers who prefer a slower pace surrounded by forest, trails, and river access.

The property combines a peaceful atmosphere with easy outdoor exploration. Guests can enjoy time on site, spend mornings watching birds, and use the lodge as a starting point for discovering more of the Tena and Amazon region.
