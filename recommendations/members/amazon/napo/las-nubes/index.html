Where is Las Nubes?

The official Las Nubes website places the property in the Cosanga valley and lists the same WhatsApp number linked here. Use that location when arranging a transfer; a Tena recommendation category is not an address.

Ask the lodge to send its current entrance pin and written arrival instructions. Match those details to the transfer quotation and the place where you will actually sleep. A map search for a business name is not enough to confirm an arrival point.

Choose the room before building the itinerary

Request the exact cabin or suite, bed layout, maximum occupancy and bathroom arrangements for your dates. Ask which meals, utilities and services are included, and which need a separate reservation. Tell the lodge about mobility needs or equipment before paying.

Compare the total stay rather than a headline nightly rate: taxes, meals, transfer costs, activity fees, payment conditions and cancellation terms can change the decision. This guide does not confirm a live price or available room.

Plan activities around access and conditions

For birdwatching, discuss target habitats, walking level, start times and whether a guide is included. Wildlife sightings are not guaranteed. Confirm current trail access and any rules for photography before setting a detailed schedule.

Treat river access and water activities as separate decisions. Ask about conditions, qualified providers, required equipment and alternatives when an outing cannot operate. Do not assume that a nearby activity is included in the room rate.

Fit the stay into the rest of Ecuador

Share the previous night’s location, the onward destination, fixed flights and the luggage you will carry. Request realistic departure and arrival windows before combining a lodge stay with a distant activity on the same day.

If your trip is centered on Tena, compare the extra journey with staying near that destination. Use the Tena planning page below to work through that choice; it does not establish a second Las Nubes property in Tena.

Questions before booking

Is this a confirmed Tena address?

No. The official lodge website identifies the Cosanga valley. Obtain the entrance pin and directions from the lodge before arranging transport.

Does this page confirm a booking?

No. The lodge must confirm the room, dates, total price, inclusions, payment and cancellation conditions.

Are guided activities included?

Ask the lodge which activities and guiding are included for your stay and which require separate arrangements.
