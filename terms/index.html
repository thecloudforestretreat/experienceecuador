Terms questions answered

Is Experience Ecuador a travel provider?

Experience Ecuador primarily provides research, planning, recommendations, and referral paths. An independent provider is responsible for a service unless a separate agreement explicitly states otherwise.

Does submitting a form create a booking?

No. A booking exists only after the responsible provider confirms availability, price, inclusions, payment, cancellation terms, and any other required conditions.

Are prices and operating details guaranteed?

No. Prices, schedules, access, weather, availability, policies, and operating conditions can change. Confirm important details with the responsible provider before relying on them.

Can recommendations include commercial partners?

Yes. Commercial participation may support visibility, but pages should remain useful and transparent. Travelers must still evaluate and confirm the provider’s current details.

May I copy content from the site?

Not for republication or commercial reuse without permission, except where applicable law permits. Contact Experience Ecuador for licensing or media questions.

What happens if these terms change?

The page may be updated as the site or requirements change. The latest published effective date identifies the current version, and mandatory rights under applicable law remain unaffected.
