Frequently asked questions

Answers before you continue

Does Experience Ecuador currently publish a star rating?

No verified aggregate rating is published on this page yet. A rating will appear only after an official review source is connected and the source, count, and context can be represented accurately.

Can I leave a review now?

You can send feedback through the Contact page. If an official public review profile becomes available, this page can link to it directly.

Are reviews for listed businesses shown here?

This page is for feedback about Experience Ecuador. Ratings or testimonials belonging to an independent business should remain on that business profile or the original review platform.

Can a partner pay for a positive review?

No. Paid visibility and editorial or traveler feedback must remain distinct. Commercial participation should be disclosed and does not guarantee a positive evaluation.

How are false or disputed reviews handled?

A disputed claim should be checked against its source and supporting context. Experience Ecuador should correct or remove content that cannot be substantiated.

How do I report a problem or correction?

Use Contact and include the exact page URL, the statement in question, relevant dates, and any supporting source. Do not include sensitive payment information.
