Clear answers

Transportation planning questions

Use these answers to avoid treating a planning estimate as a confirmed service or guaranteed schedule.

Are the transfer times guaranteed?

No. They are planning estimates. Traffic, road works, weather, pickup logistics, stops, and the selected route can change the actual time.

Does the estimator provide confirmed prices?

Not yet. Routes without verified commercial pricing display Coming soon. Request a trip plan for a current, operator-confirmed transport option.

Should I add extra time before a flight?

Yes. Build in a buffer appropriate to the airport, route, time of day, weather, and airline check-in requirements rather than treating a road estimate as a departure guarantee.

How should I choose between a car and an SUV?

Match the vehicle to passengers, full-size luggage, equipment, mobility needs, child seats, road type, and comfort. Confirm actual capacity with the operator.

Are stops and waiting time included?

Not automatically. Ask whether planned stops, flight delays, waiting time, parking, tolls, and pickup changes are included in the confirmed quote.

Can Experience Ecuador confirm a transfer booking?

The estimator itself cannot. A service is confirmed only when the responsible provider verifies route, vehicle, date, pickup, inclusions, availability, price, payment, and cancellation terms.
