What a useful estimate includes

We publish only planning information that can be explained clearly. Commercial prices will appear only after route, vehicle, inclusions, and operator availability are verified.

What a confirmed quote should cover
  • Pickup point, route, date, and approximate departure time
  • Vehicle capacity for passengers and luggage
  • Included waiting time, stops, and any special equipment
  • Current operator availability and final price in USD

Clear answers

Transportation planning questions

Use these answers to avoid treating a planning estimate as a confirmed service or guaranteed schedule.

Are the transfer times guaranteed?

No. They are planning estimates. Traffic, road works, weather, pickup logistics, stops, and the selected route can change the actual time.

Does the estimator provide confirmed prices?

Not yet. Routes without verified commercial pricing display “Coming soon.” Request a trip plan for a current, operator-confirmed transport option.

Should I add extra time before a flight?

Yes. Build in a buffer appropriate to the airport, route, time of day, weather, and airline check-in requirements. Never treat a road estimate as a departure guarantee.
