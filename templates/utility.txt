UTILITY / SUPPORT TEMPLATE

PURPOSE
Utility / Support pages are not SEO drivers, but they are CRITICAL for:
- trust
- conversions
- user confidence
- legal compliance
- reducing friction

These pages support the ecosystem.

They include:
- FAQ
- Contact
- Transportation
- Privacy Policy
- Terms
- Partners
- Join / Become a Member

CORE UX GOALS
1. Remove user doubts
2. Provide clear answers quickly
3. Build trust
4. Support conversion indirectly
5. Keep layout clean and distraction-free

DESIGN PRINCIPLE
These pages should feel:
- clean
- simple
- direct
- easy to scan

NOT:
- overly designed
- cluttered
- marketing-heavy

-----------------------------------

COMMON STRUCTURE

1. Hero (minimal)
2. Page Content (structured blocks)
3. CTA (optional depending on page)

-----------------------------------

1. HERO

Purpose:
- establish page purpose quickly

Content:
- H1
- 1 short sentence

Example:
H1: Frequently Asked Questions
Copy: Answers to common questions about planning a trip to Ecuador.

Rules:
- very compact
- no large images required
- no wasted space

-----------------------------------

2. PAGE CONTENT STRUCTURE

This varies by page type:

-----------------------------------

FAQ PAGE

Structure:
- grouped questions by category

Categories:
- Planning
- Regions
- Experiences
- Logistics

Each item:
- question (bold or expandable)
- short answer

Rules:
- scannable
- no long paragraphs
- optional accordion UI

SEO:
- strong FAQ schema opportunity

-----------------------------------

CONTACT PAGE

Structure:
- short intro
- contact methods

Include:
- email
- form (optional)
- response expectations

Optional:
- WhatsApp or messaging CTA

Tone:
- friendly
- direct

-----------------------------------

TRANSPORTATION PAGE

Structure:
- overview
- transport types

Sections:
- flights
- private transport
- buses
- internal travel tips

Include:
- practical advice
- internal links to regions

-----------------------------------

PRIVACY POLICY / TERMS

Structure:
- simple legal layout

Rules:
- clean typography
- readable spacing
- no design distractions

-----------------------------------

PARTNERS PAGE

Purpose:
- showcase collaborations

Structure:
- intro
- partner logos or cards
- short descriptions

Optional:
- link to member pages

-----------------------------------

JOIN / BECOME A MEMBER

Purpose:
- monetization

Structure:
- value proposition
- benefits
- who it is for
- CTA

Sections:
- why join
- what you get
- how it works
- CTA button

Tone:
- clear and persuasive
- not aggressive

-----------------------------------

3. CTA (OPTIONAL)

Used on:
- FAQ
- Contact
- Join

Examples:
- Start Planning Your Trip
- Explore Regions
- Apply to Join

-----------------------------------

SPACING RULES

- minimal vertical spacing
- no empty sections
- tight layout
- prioritize readability

-----------------------------------

MOBILE-FIRST RULES

- stacked layout
- short sections
- easy tap targets
- collapsible FAQs

-----------------------------------

DESKTOP RULES

- narrow content width
- centered layout
- readable line length

-----------------------------------

SEO REQUIREMENTS

Only apply where relevant:
- FAQ page (high SEO value)
- Transportation page (medium SEO value)

Include:
- proper headings
- internal links

-----------------------------------

INTERNAL LINKING

Use these pages to link back to:
- regions
- experiences
- planning pages
- trip builder

-----------------------------------

CONTENT TONE

- clear
- helpful
- trustworthy
- concise

-----------------------------------

FINAL NOTE

These pages should feel like:
- support infrastructure
- trust builders

Not:
- marketing pages
- content-heavy SEO pages
