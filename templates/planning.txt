PLANNING TEMPLATE (TRIP BUILDER / PLANNING HUB)

PURPOSE
The Planning template is your highest conversion layer outside of the Trip Builder tool itself.

It should:
- guide users toward building a trip
- reduce decision fatigue
- connect all major content layers (regions, experiences, guides, members)
- act as a bridge between research and action

This template will power:
- /trip-builder
- /plan-your-trip
- itinerary pages
- planning guides

CORE UX GOALS
1. Help users make decisions quickly
2. Provide structured guidance
3. Reduce overwhelm
4. Drive users into Trip Builder or key pages
5. Support SEO for planning-related queries

SECTION ORDER

1. Hero
2. Planning Overview
3. Choose Your Travel Style
4. Choose Your Regions
5. Choose Your Experiences
6. Sample Itineraries
7. Planning Tips
8. Key Travel Guides
9. Trip Builder CTA
10. FAQ
11. Final CTA

1. HERO
Purpose:
- set expectation
- guide user into planning mindset

Content:
- H1
- short supporting text
- CTA

Example:
H1: Plan Your Ecuador Trip
Copy: Build your itinerary by choosing regions, experiences, and travel style that match your goals.

CTA:
- Start Trip Builder

Keep compact and action-focused.

2. PLANNING OVERVIEW
Purpose:
- explain how the site helps

Content:
- 2 to 3 short paragraphs

Explain:
- how to approach Ecuador planning
- importance of regions + experiences
- how the platform helps simplify decisions

3. CHOOSE YOUR TRAVEL STYLE
Purpose:
- segment users early

Content:
- cards for styles

Examples:
- Adventure
- Luxury
- Family
- Wildlife
- Cultural

4. CHOOSE YOUR REGIONS
Purpose:
- guide geographic decisions

Content:
- region cards

Regions:
- Andes
- Amazon
- Coast
- Galapagos

5. CHOOSE YOUR EXPERIENCES
Purpose:
- guide activity decisions

Content:
- experience cards

6. SAMPLE ITINERARIES
Purpose:
- give users real examples

7. PLANNING TIPS
Purpose:
- practical value

8. KEY TRAVEL GUIDES
Purpose:
- internal linking

9. TRIP BUILDER CTA
Purpose:
- main conversion

10. FAQ

11. FINAL CTA

DESIGN PRINCIPLE
This page should feel like:
- a planning assistant
- a structured guide
- a conversion engine
