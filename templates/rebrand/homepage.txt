HOME TEMPLATE

PURPOSE
The homepage is the master entry point for the Experience Ecuador platform.
It must act as:
- a search hub
- a planning hub
- a discovery engine
- a conversion funnel

It should immediately communicate that the site helps travelers research Ecuador, compare regions and experiences, discover curated recommendations, and plan a more realistic trip.

CORE UX GOALS
1. Establish trust quickly
2. Show the breadth of Ecuador without overwhelming the user
3. Route users into the right next step
4. Support internal linking to major SEO page clusters
5. Drive users toward trip planning, destination exploration, and member discovery

SECTION ORDER

1. Hero
2. Value Proposition Strip
3. Regions Gateway
4. Experiences Gateway
5. Planning Paths
6. Featured Recommendations / Members
7. Spotlights
8. Why Use Experience Ecuador
9. Trip Builder CTA
10. SEO Support Content
11. FAQ
12. Final CTA

1. HERO
Purpose:
- communicate the brand promise
- establish site purpose
- provide immediate next steps

Content:
- eyebrow / short label
- H1
- 1 to 2 sentence supporting paragraph
- primary CTA
- secondary CTA
- one strong supporting image or video

Recommended structure:
- H1 should target broad Ecuador travel intent
- primary CTA should route to trip builder
- secondary CTA should route to regions or recommendations

Example content structure:
- Eyebrow: Plan Ecuador with more clarity
- H1: Explore Ecuador by Region, Experience, and Travel Style
- Copy: Research destinations, compare experiences, discover curated recommendations, and build a more realistic Ecuador itinerary.
- CTA 1: Start Planning Your Trip
- CTA 2: Explore Regions

Image rules:
- one strong hero visual
- can be image or muted video background if performance allows
- avoid giant empty cinematic spacing

Mobile rules:
- stacked layout
- compact headline and CTAs
- image below or behind copy
- no oversized hero height

Desktop rules:
- split layout or centered overlay
- content width controlled
- no wasted whitespace

2. VALUE PROPOSITION STRIP
Purpose:
- immediately explain what makes the site different

Content:
- 3 to 4 compact value cards

Suggested cards:
- Research destinations
- Compare experiences
- Find curated recommendations
- Build a realistic itinerary

Each card should be short, scannable, and linked when useful.

3. REGIONS GATEWAY
Purpose:
- route users into the geographic structure
- support internal linking

Content:
- H2
- intro sentence
- 4 major region cards

Regions:
- Andes
- Amazon
- Coast
- Galapagos

Each card should include:
- image
- region name
- one-line positioning
- CTA to region page

This section is a major homepage SEO and navigation block.

4. EXPERIENCES GATEWAY
Purpose:
- route users into the experience layer
- support users who think by activity instead of geography

Content:
- H2
- 6 experience cards or compact tiles

Experiences:
- Wildlife & Birding
- Nature
- Adventure
- Culture
- Relaxation
- Culinary

Each card:
- image or icon
- title
- short value statement
- CTA to experience page

This section should visually differ from the regions section but feel connected.

5. PLANNING PATHS
Purpose:
- give users multiple routes into the site based on intent

Content:
- H2
- 3 to 4 path cards

Suggested paths:
- First-time Ecuador traveler
- Family trip planning
- Luxury Ecuador travel
- Private tours and custom planning

Each card should link to:
- collection pages
- travel guides
- itinerary pages

This block helps route broad homepage traffic into high-value SEO pages.

6. FEATURED RECOMMENDATIONS / MEMBERS
Purpose:
- support monetization
- showcase curated businesses without turning the homepage into a directory

Content:
- H2
- 3 to 6 featured member cards

Each card:
- image
- business name
- location
- category
- short summary
- best-for label
- CTA to member page

Only use curated featured members.
Keep the block visually premium and limited.

7. SPOTLIGHTS
Purpose:
- add freshness
- feature events, seasonal moments, or current reasons to visit

Content:
- H2
- 2 to 4 spotlight cards
- CTA to /spotlights and /es/destacados

Examples:
- seasonal wildlife moments
- regional festivals
- holiday travel windows
- limited-time travel highlights

This should feel timely, not like a blog feed.

8. WHY USE EXPERIENCE ECUADOR
Purpose:
- clarify the platform’s role
- differentiate from generic travel content

Content:
- H2
- 3 to 5 short blocks or cards

Suggested themes:
- curated over cluttered
- realistic trip planning
- region + experience based discovery
- local context and practical decision support

This is a trust and differentiation section.

9. TRIP BUILDER CTA
Purpose:
- push users into your main conversion tool

Content:
- compact section with:
  - H2
  - 1 short paragraph
  - primary CTA
  - optional secondary CTA

Suggested CTA:
- Build Your Ecuador Trip
- Explore Sample Itineraries

This should be one of the strongest CTA sections on the homepage.

10. SEO SUPPORT CONTENT
Purpose:
- give the homepage enough contextual depth to support broad ranking intent
- reinforce internal linking

Content:
- H2
- 2 to 4 short paragraphs
- internal links to:
  - regions
  - experiences
  - travel guide pages
  - trip builder
  - recommendations

This should be concise, useful, and natural.
Do not turn the homepage into a long article.

11. FAQ
Purpose:
- support SEO
- answer broad traveler questions
- add schema value later

Suggested questions:
- What is the best way to plan a trip to Ecuador?
- How many days do you need in Ecuador?
- Can you combine Galapagos with mainland Ecuador?
- What regions should first-time travelers visit?
- Where can I find trusted recommendations in Ecuador?

Keep answers direct and scannable.

12. FINAL CTA
Purpose:
- end with a clear action
- avoid dead page endings

Content:
- headline
- one support sentence
- 2 CTAs

Suggested CTAs:
- Start Your Trip Plan
- Explore Ecuador Regions

IMAGE RULES
- hero: 1 image or video
- region cards: 4 images
- experience cards: 6 images or strong visuals
- featured members: 3 to 6 images
- spotlights: 2 to 4 images

Use placeholder images for now where needed.
Keep all media functional and tied to decision-making.

SPACING RULES
- no dead whitespace
- tight vertical rhythm
- strong section adjacency
- mobile spacing compressed
- avoid oversized top and bottom padding
- every section should feel connected to the next

MOBILE-FIRST RULES
- single column first
- short paragraphs
- cards stack naturally
- CTA buttons full width or 2-up only when appropriate
- keep grids simple
- avoid horizontal overflow
- maintain readable text sizes and spacing

DESKTOP RULES
- controlled max-width
- 2 to 4 column grids only where useful
- avoid over-stretching content
- keep the eye moving through clear section hierarchy

SEO REQUIREMENTS
Every homepage implementation should include:
- optimized title tag
- optimized meta description
- canonical
- OG title
- OG description
- OG image
- Twitter image
- structured H1 and H2 hierarchy
- FAQ schema
- breadcrumb schema only if needed
- strong internal linking to priority pages

CONVERSION REQUIREMENTS
Homepage must include CTA links to:
- trip builder
- regions
- experiences
- collection / SEO landing pages
- featured members
- spotlights

Do not let the homepage become a passive brochure.
It must actively route users deeper into the site.

INTERNAL LINKING REQUIREMENTS
The homepage should directly link to:
- all 4 region pages
- all 6 experience root pages
- 3 to 4 priority collection pages
- featured member pages
- spotlights hub
- trip builder

PRIORITY COLLECTION LINKS TO FEATURE
Recommended to feature from homepage:
- /ecuador-birdwatching-tours/
- /ecuador-cloud-forest-lodges/
- /ecuador-private-tours/
- /ecuador-luxury-travel/

CONTENT TONE
- confident
- inspiring
- useful
- practical
- high-end but not elitist
- mobile-friendly
- search-friendly

DESIGN PRINCIPLE
This homepage should feel like:
- a premium travel planning platform
- a decision engine
- a curated Ecuador discovery hub

Not:
- a generic tourism brochure
- a cluttered directory
- a giant text wall
