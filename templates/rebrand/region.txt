REGION TEMPLATE

PURPOSE
The Region page is one of the most important SEO and UX layers on the site.

It must:
- rank for major geographic queries (Andes, Amazon, Coast, Galapagos)
- act as a hub for destinations, experiences, and planning
- guide users into deeper pages (destinations, experiences, members, guides)
- help users understand what makes each region unique

Each Region page should feel like a high-level guide + navigation system.

CORE UX GOALS
1. Quickly explain the region
2. Help users decide if the region fits their trip
3. Route users into destinations and experiences
4. Support strong internal linking
5. Encourage trip planning

SECTION ORDER

1. Hero
2. Region Overview
3. Key Highlights
4. Destinations in This Region
5. Experiences in This Region
6. Recommended Members (Curated)
7. Sample Itinerary Block
8. Planning Tips
9. Related Guides / Blog
10. Trip Builder CTA
11. FAQ
12. Final CTA

1. HERO
Purpose:
- introduce the region
- establish visual identity

Content:
- H1 (region-focused keyword)
- short supporting paragraph
- 1 strong image

Example:
H1: Explore the Andes Region of Ecuador
Copy: Discover high-altitude landscapes, volcanoes, colonial cities, and diverse outdoor experiences across Ecuador’s Andes.

Rules:
- keep height controlled
- no excessive empty space
- mobile-first layout

2. REGION OVERVIEW
Purpose:
- give users a clear understanding of the region

Content:
- 2 to 3 short paragraphs
- internal links to:
  - destinations
  - experiences
  - guides

Topics to cover:
- geography
- travel vibe
- why visit
- how it compares to other regions

Keep it concise and scannable.

3. KEY HIGHLIGHTS
Purpose:
- quick scannable summary

Content:
- 4 to 6 highlight cards or bullets

Examples:
- best for wildlife
- best for culture
- best for adventure
- accessibility
- typical trip length

This helps users quickly decide if the region fits them.

4. DESTINATIONS IN THIS REGION
Purpose:
- push users into destination pages

Content:
- H2
- grid of destination cards

Each card:
- image
- destination name
- short description
- CTA to destination page

Examples:
Andes:
- Quito
- Cotopaxi
- Baños
- Otavalo

This is a major internal linking block.

5. EXPERIENCES IN THIS REGION
Purpose:
- connect region to activities

Content:
- H2
- experience cards filtered to region

Examples:
- hiking
- birdwatching
- cultural tours
- adventure sports

Each card:
- title
- short description
- CTA to experience page

6. RECOMMENDED MEMBERS (CURATED)
Purpose:
- monetization + trust

Content:
- 3 to 6 curated listings

Each listing:
- image
- name
- location
- category
- short summary
- CTA

Keep curated, not directory-like.

7. SAMPLE ITINERARY BLOCK
Purpose:
- help users visualize a trip

Content:
- example 3 to 7 day itinerary

Format:
Day 1: Location + activity
Day 2: Location + activity
...

Include:
- short descriptions
- internal links where possible

This is high-value for SEO and UX.

8. PLANNING TIPS
Purpose:
- practical travel info

Content:
- 4 to 6 short tips

Examples:
- best time to visit
- altitude considerations
- travel time between locations
- packing advice

Keep concise.

9. RELATED GUIDES / BLOG
Purpose:
- internal linking to long-form content

Content:
- 3 to 6 guide links

Examples:
- Quito travel guide
- packing list
- itineraries

This helps SEO depth.

10. TRIP BUILDER CTA
Purpose:
- push conversion

Content:
- H2
- short paragraph
- CTA

Example:
Build your Ecuador itinerary based on your preferred regions and experiences.

11. FAQ
Purpose:
- SEO + user clarity

Suggested questions:
- How many days should I spend in this region?
- What is the best time to visit?
- What are the top destinations?
- Is this region good for first-time visitors?

Keep answers short and useful.

12. FINAL CTA
Purpose:
- close the page strongly

Content:
- headline
- 1 sentence
- 2 CTAs

Example:
- Explore Destinations
- Start Planning

IMAGE RULES
- hero: 1 image
- destinations: 4 to 8 images
- experiences: 4 to 6 images
- members: 3 to 6 images

Avoid decorative-only images.

SPACING RULES
- no large empty gaps
- tight vertical rhythm
- consistent section spacing
- mobile optimized spacing

MOBILE-FIRST RULES
- single column layout
- stacked cards
- readable text
- full-width CTAs

DESKTOP RULES
- grid layouts where appropriate
- controlled max width
- avoid stretched content

SEO REQUIREMENTS
Each Region page should include:
- keyword-optimized title
- meta description
- canonical
- H1 with region keyword
- H2 structure for sections
- internal links to:
  - destinations
  - experiences
  - guides
  - trip builder

CONVERSION REQUIREMENTS
Include CTAs to:
- trip builder
- destinations
- experiences
- members

INTERNAL LINKING
Each region page should link to:
- all its destinations
- related experiences
- 2 to 4 guides
- trip builder
- selected members

CONTENT TONE
- informative
- inspiring
- practical
- easy to scan

DESIGN PRINCIPLE
The Region page should feel like:
- a smart travel guide
- a navigation hub
- a decision-making tool

Not:
- a blog post
- a directory
- a wall of text
