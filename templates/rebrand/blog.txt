BLOG / GUIDE PAGE TEMPLATE

SECTION ORDER

1. Hero
2. Intro (Hook + Context)
3. Quick Summary / Key Takeaways
4. Main Content Sections
5. Internal Linking Blocks (Inline)
6. Visual Break Sections (Optional)
7. Related Destinations / Experiences
8. Related Collections
9. FAQ
10. Final CTA

---

1. HERO SECTION
- H1 (SEO optimized title)
- 1 short supporting sentence
- Author or brand line (optional)
- 1 hero image

---

2. INTRO (HOOK + CONTEXT)
- 2 to 3 paragraphs
- Answer the intent quickly
- Set expectations

---

3. QUICK SUMMARY / KEY TAKEAWAYS
- 4 to 6 bullets
- Fast answers for skimmers

---

4. MAIN CONTENT SECTIONS
- H2 and H3 structure
- Short paragraphs (2–4 lines max)
- Lists and bullets preferred
- Clear subtopics

---

5. INTERNAL LINKING (INLINE)
- Link naturally to:
  - Destination pages
  - Experience pages
  - Collection pages
  - Member pages

---

6. VISUAL BREAK SECTIONS (OPTIONAL)
- Image or highlight block
- Used every 2–3 sections to avoid fatigue

---

7. RELATED DESTINATIONS / EXPERIENCES
- 3 to 6 cards linking deeper

---

8. RELATED COLLECTIONS
- 2 to 4 links to high-level pages

---

9. FAQ
- 4 to 6 questions
- Target long-tail queries

---

10. FINAL CTA
- Headline
- 1 line support
- CTA to:
  - Trip Builder
  - Collections
  - Recommendations

---

IMAGE RULES
- Hero: 1 image
- Optional images throughout
- Keep lightweight for performance

---

SPACING RULES
- Tight paragraphs
- No large empty gaps
- Consistent rhythm

---

SEO REQUIREMENTS
- Title tag
- Meta description
- Canonical
- OG tags
- Structured headings (H1-H3)
- FAQ schema
- Internal linking

---

CONTENT RULES
- Answer first, explain second
- Avoid fluff
- Use scannable structure
- Focus on intent

---

CONVERSION ROLE
- Push users into:
  - Destinations
  - Experiences
  - Members
  - Trip Builder
