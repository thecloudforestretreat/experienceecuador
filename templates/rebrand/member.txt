MEMBER PAGE TEMPLATE

SECTION ORDER

1. Hero
2. Trust + Key Info Strip
3. Overview
4. Gallery / Media
5. Experience / Offering Breakdown
6. Why Choose This Member
7. Location Context
8. Reviews / Social Proof (Optional)
9. Related Experiences
10. Related Destinations
11. FAQ
12. Final CTA

---

1. HERO SECTION
- Business Name (H1)
- Location + Category
- 1–2 sentence positioning
- Primary CTA (Visit Website / Contact)
- Secondary CTA (WhatsApp / Inquiry)
- 3 highlights (best for, location, unique factor)
- 1 strong image

---

2. TRUST + KEY INFO STRIP
- Quick facts:
  - Location
  - Category (Lodge, Tour, Hotel, etc.)
  - Price range (optional)
  - Best for
  - Duration / offering type

---

3. OVERVIEW
- H2
- 2 to 4 paragraphs
- Clear positioning
- What makes this business unique

---

4. GALLERY / MEDIA
- 4 to 8 images (or video if available)
- Real visuals of experience

---

5. EXPERIENCE / OFFERING BREAKDOWN
- H2
- Sections or cards:
  - What they offer
  - Packages or services
  - Key highlights

---

6. WHY CHOOSE THIS MEMBER
- H2
- 3 to 5 bullets:
  - Unique differentiators
  - Value points
  - Experience quality

---

7. LOCATION CONTEXT
- H2
- Where it is located
- Distance from key destinations
- Map or directional context

---

8. REVIEWS / SOCIAL PROOF (OPTIONAL)
- Testimonials or short quotes
- Keep concise

---

9. RELATED EXPERIENCES
- Links to:
  - Experience pages relevant to this member

---

10. RELATED DESTINATIONS
- Links to:
  - Destination pages
  - Region pages

---

11. FAQ
- 3 to 5 questions
Examples:
- What is included?
- How do I book?
- Is it family-friendly?

---

12. FINAL CTA
- Strong conversion push
- 2 CTAs:
  - Visit Website
  - Contact / WhatsApp

---

IMAGE RULES
- Hero: 1 image
- Gallery: multiple images
- Use real images whenever possible

---

SPACING RULES
- Tight layout
- No empty gaps
- Content flows directly

---

SEO REQUIREMENTS
- Title tag (Business + Location + Category)
- Meta description
- Canonical
- OG tags
- Structured data (LocalBusiness recommended)
- Internal linking

---

CONVERSION REQUIREMENTS
- Click tracking:
  - Website click
  - WhatsApp click
  - Email click
- CTA in hero
- CTA in final section
