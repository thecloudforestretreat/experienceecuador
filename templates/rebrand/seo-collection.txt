COLLECTION / SEO LANDING PAGE TEMPLATE

SECTION ORDER

1. Hero
2. Quick Answer Strip
3. SEO Intro
4. Top Regions / Destinations
5. Experience Layer
6. Featured Members or Standardized Recommended Routes
7. Comparison / How to Choose
8. Who This Is For
9. Related Planning Paths
10. FAQ
11. Final CTA

---

1. HERO
- Kicker
- H1
- 2 to 3 sentence intro
- CTA 1
- CTA 2
- 3 hero pills
- 1 strong hero image

---

2. QUICK ANSWER STRIP
- 3 to 5 cards
- Help users choose quickly
- Common fields:
  - Best for
  - Top regions
  - Trip style
  - Travel style
  - Next step

---

3. SEO INTRO
- H2
- 2 to 4 paragraphs
- Must explain how to choose
- Must include internal links to:
  - Trip Builder
  - Relevant region pages
  - Relevant experience pages
  - Best Places to Visit in Ecuador
  - Where to Stay in Ecuador

---

4. TOP REGIONS / DESTINATIONS
- 3 to 6 cards
Each card:
- image
- region or destination name
- short comparison description
- CTA link

---

5. EXPERIENCE LAYER (MANDATORY)
- 3 to 4 cards
- Link to confirmed experience hubs
- Short explanation per experience
- Must appear after Top Regions / Destinations

---

6. FEATURED MEMBERS OR STANDARDIZED RECOMMENDED ROUTES
- 3 to 6 cards
- If real inventory exists, use it
- If not, standardize as:
  - Recommended Routes
  - Sample Planning Paths
  - Curated Route Paths
- Must help users compare real route logic
- Do NOT invent fake inventory

---

7. COMPARISON / HOW TO CHOOSE
- 3 to 4 cards or blocks
Typical logic:
- Best for deeper immersion
- Best for easier access
- Best for comfort
- Best for private planning
- Use decision language:
  - Best for...
  - Choose this if...
  - Best when...

---

8. WHO THIS IS FOR
- 4 to 5 audience-fit cards
Typical audiences:
- Couples
- Families
- Wildlife travelers
- First-time travelers
- Luxury travelers

---

9. RELATED PLANNING PATHS
- Related collection pages
- Related guide pages
- Trip Builder
- Best Places to Visit in Ecuador
- Where to Stay in Ecuador
- Must strengthen internal linking
- Must include cross-collection linking where relevant

---

10. FAQ
- 4 to 6 questions
- Never exceed 6
- Must align with real search intent
- Must support FAQ schema

---

11. FINAL CTA
- Headline
- One supporting line
- 2 CTA buttons
