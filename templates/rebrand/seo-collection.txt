COLLECTION / SEO LANDING PAGE TEMPLATE

SECTION ORDER

1. Hero
2. Quick Answer Strip
3. SEO Intro
4. Top Regions / Destinations
5. Experience Layer
6. Featured Members
7. Comparison / How to Choose
8. Who This Is For
9. Related Planning Paths
10. FAQ
11. Final CTA

---

1. HERO
- Kicker
- H1
- 2 to 3 sentence intro
- CTA 1
- CTA 2
- 3 hero pills
- 1 strong hero image

---

2. QUICK ANSWER STRIP
- 3 to 5 cards
- Help users choose quickly
- Common fields:
  - Best for
  - Top regions
  - Trip length
  - Travel style
  - Next step

---

3. SEO INTRO
- H2
- 2 to 4 paragraphs
- Must explain how to choose
- Must include internal links to:
  - Trip Builder
  - Relevant region pages
  - Relevant experience pages

---

4. TOP REGIONS / DESTINATIONS
- 3 to 6 cards
Each card:
- Image
- Region or destination name
- Short comparison description
- CTA link

---

5. EXPERIENCE LAYER
- REQUIRED
- 4 cards is typical
- Usually:
  - Nature
  - Wildlife and Birding
  - Adventure
  - Relaxation

---

6. FEATURED MEMBERS
- 3 to 6 cards
- Can be:
  - lodge
  - guide
  - curated path
  - recommendation layer
- Must help users compare real options

---

7. COMPARISON / HOW TO CHOOSE
- 3 to 4 cards or blocks
Typical logic:
- Best for deeper immersion
- Best for easier access
- Best for comfort
- Best for private planning

---

8. WHO THIS IS FOR
- 4 to 5 audience-fit cards
Typical audiences:
- Couples
- Families
- Wildlife travelers
- First-time travelers
- Luxury travelers

---

9. RELATED PLANNING PATHS
- Related collection pages
- Related guide pages
- Trip Builder
- Must strengthen internal linking

---

10. FAQ
- 4 to 6 questions
- Must align with real search intent
- Must support FAQ schema

---

11. FINAL CTA
- Headline
- One supporting line
- 2 CTA buttons

---

NON-NEGOTIABLE RULES
- No weather
- No when-to-visit section
- No seasonal breakdowns
- No best months
- No climate summaries
- No forecast widgets
- Use only global CSS:
  - /assets/css/header.css
  - /assets/css/footer.css
  - /assets/css/site.css
- Use /assets/js/site.js
- No <style> blocks
- No page-level CSS
- No page-specific shared-component JS
- Use header/footer mount pattern
- Spanish visible text must use full accents
- URLs must remain ASCII-safe
- Use placeholder image only when a real image is missing:
  /assets/images/EE-OG-Image-1.jpg
- OG image always:
  https://experienceecuador.com/assets/images/EE-OG-Image-1.jpg
