3. SEO INTRO
- H2

- Include keywords naturally
- Internal links to:

---

4. TOP REGIONS / DESTINATIONS
- H2
- 3 to 6 cards
Each card:
- Image
- Name
- Short description
- CTA link

5. FEATURED MEMBERS
- H2
- 3 to 6 curated cards
Each card:
- Image
- Name
- Location
- Category
- Short summary
- Best for
- CTA to member page

---

6. COMPARISON / HOW TO CHOOSE
- H2
- 2 to 4 comparison blocks
Examples:
- Best for families

- Best for nature
- Best for access

7. WHO THIS IS FOR
- H2
- 4 to 5 audience segments
Examples:
- Couples
- Families
- Birdwatchers
- First-time travelers

---

8. RELATED PLANNING PATHS
- Links to:
  - 2 to 4 collection pages

  - Trip Builder

9. FAQ
- H2
- 4 to 6 questions

10. FINAL CTA
- Short headline
- 1 supporting line
- 2 CTA buttons
