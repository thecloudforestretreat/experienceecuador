Frequently asked questions

Answers before you continue

What is Experience Ecuador?

Experience Ecuador is a bilingual Ecuador travel research, planning, and recommendation platform. It connects regional guides, itinerary tools, practical advice, and local businesses in one planning journey.

Is Experience Ecuador a booking website?

The site helps travelers research and plan. Some pages may refer travelers to a provider or planning service, but a booking exists only after the responsible provider confirms the service, price, availability, and terms.

How many days do I need for Ecuador?

A focused mainland trip can work in seven days. Ten to fourteen days gives more room for two regions or a mainland and Galápagos combination. Protect fixed transport and avoid adding distant regions without transfer buffers.

How are recommendations selected?

A recommendation should be relevant to the page, useful to the traveler, supported by accurate business information, and transparent about commercial participation. Travelers should still confirm current operating details directly.

Can a local business be listed?

Yes. Ecuadorian businesses can review the partner program and contact the team. The platform is designed to support free, monthly, and annual participation while keeping usefulness and disclosure central.

How can I request help or report a correction?

Use the contact page and include the relevant URL, dates, group details, or exact correction. Do not send sensitive payment information through a general inquiry.
