The short answer

Can you visit the Amazon and Galapagos in one trip?

Yes. A well-paced first itinerary needs about 12–14 days: a Quito arrival buffer, 3–4 nights in an Amazon lodge, another protected connection night when necessary, and 5–7 nights in Galapagos. The key is not the order—it is avoiding a fragile same-day connection between a river or road transfer and a fixed island flight.

Amazon and Galapagos itinerary questions

Which should come first?

Use the order that offers confirmed availability and the safest buffer before the most expensive fixed departure.

Is ten days enough?

It is possible but usually compressed. Twelve to fourteen days better protects transfers and gives both ecosystems meaningful time.

Is this route suitable for every mobility level?

No single version is. Boat transfers, wet landings, trails, altitude, and lodge access vary, so disclose mobility needs before choosing products.
