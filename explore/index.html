Frequently asked questions

Questions about the affiliated network

Are all six websites owned by the same company?

They are presented as an affiliated local network. Each site has its own role, content, offers, and direct contact or booking path.

Where should I begin planning an Ecuador trip?

Begin with Experience Ecuador to compare regions, timing, routes, and travel styles. Move to a specialist site when your itinerary requires deeper local expertise.

Does following a link create a booking?

No. A booking exists only after the responsible provider confirms the service, availability, price, inclusions, payment, and terms.

Which site is best for birdwatching?

Mindo Bird Watching is the specialist birding funnel. Experience Ecuador provides broader national context and route planning around that specialist experience.

Which site should I use for the Amazon?

Use Experience the Amazon for deeper Amazon-focused research and provider context after using Experience Ecuador to place the rainforest block within a national itinerary.

Are referrals and commercial relationships tracked?

Outbound clicks may carry source and attribution context so the network can understand referrals. The destination site applies its own privacy and booking practices.
