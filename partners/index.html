What does an Experience Ecuador partnership provide?

The partner program gives relevant Ecuadorian businesses a structured profile and contextual visibility inside destination, experience, and planning content. The model is designed to support free, monthly, and annual participation while keeping business facts accurate, commercial placement transparent, and traveler contact attributable.

Frequently asked questions

Answers before you continue

Who can apply to become a partner?

Ecuadorian lodges, hotels, guides, tour operators, restaurants, community projects, transport providers, and other relevant travel services can apply. Eligibility and fit are reviewed before publication.

Is there a free option?

The program is designed to support a free participation path as well as monthly and annual options. The current application should state what is included and any applicable terms.

Does payment guarantee a recommendation or ranking?

No. Commercial participation does not replace relevance, accuracy, safe practices, or useful traveler context. Paid placement should be identified clearly.

How are referrals measured?

Descriptive link events can distinguish profile views, website referrals, WhatsApp actions, inquiry starts, and the page context that produced the action, subject to user consent and available analytics.

What information should a business provide?

Provide the legal or public business name, location, website, contact person, services, languages, images you have permission to use, and accurate operating details.

How quickly will a profile be published?

No publication time is promised on this page. Timing depends on completeness, eligibility, content review, and the current publishing schedule.
