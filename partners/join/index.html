Before you apply

Partner application questions

Does an application guarantee publication?

No. Every submission is reviewed for accuracy, traveler value, regional fit, and current program capacity. Publication, placement, timing, traffic, and leads are not guaranteed.

What participation options may be available?

The program is designed to support free, monthly, and annual participation, with separately scoped opportunities when appropriate. The current offer is confirmed directly after review.

Do I need English and Spanish descriptions?

Submitting both is helpful, but the fields are optional. Accurate source information matters more than machine-translated marketing copy.

What images should I provide?

Provide clear, current images that represent the business and services accurately. Only submit images you own or are authorized to publish.

Can I update information after applying?

Yes. Use the contact page and identify the business and application email. Material operating, pricing, contact, or service changes should be reported promptly.

How are commercial relationships disclosed?

Paid or enhanced participation should be identified appropriately while recommendations remain useful and relevant to the traveler’s planning context.
