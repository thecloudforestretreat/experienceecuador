Before you send

Contact and Planning Questions

These answers clarify what to send, how requests are routed, and what the form does not confirm.

What should I include for trip-planning help?

Include your travel dates or date range, number of travelers, preferred regions, interests, approximate trip length, and any mobility or lodging priorities.

Can local businesses request a recommendation profile?

Yes. Relevant Ecuador-based lodges, guides, operators, restaurants, retreats, and other businesses can submit their offering for editorial and commercial review.

Does sending the form confirm a booking?

No. The form starts a conversation; it does not confirm prices, availability, reservations, transportation, or tour services.

Can I write in Spanish?

Yes. Experience Ecuador accepts inquiries in English and Spanish. Use the language in which you can explain your request most clearly.

Where should media requests go?

Select Media or Partnerships and include your organization, the scope of the request, timing, requested deliverable, and relevant links.

How is form information used?

It is used to respond to the inquiry and route it appropriately. Read the privacy policy for details about data handling.
