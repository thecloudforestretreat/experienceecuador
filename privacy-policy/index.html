Policy details

What the policy covers

These sections preserve the operational policy while making the main responsibilities easier to scan.

Information we collect

We may receive contact, trip, business, and message details you submit. Technical logs and analytics may include device, browser, referring page, interactions, approximate location, UTM parameters, and advertising click identifiers.

How information is used

Information supports replies, planning requests, partner review, site operation, security, measurement, error diagnosis, content improvement, and attribution. First-touch and recent-touch attribution may be retained for up to 90 days.

Cookies and analytics

Cookies, local storage, tags, and similar technologies may support preferences, measurement, attribution, fraud prevention, and service operation. Available browser and consent controls can limit some technologies.

Service providers and referrals

Hosting, analytics, form, security, communications, and other providers may process limited data for us. External businesses reached through a referral apply their own privacy and booking policies.

Retention and protection

We keep information only as long as reasonably needed for the purpose collected, legal obligations, dispute handling, security, and recordkeeping. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

Choices and contact

You may ask about access, correction, deletion, or other applicable choices. We may need enough information to verify and locate the relevant record. Children under 13 should not submit personal information.

Frequently asked questions

Privacy questions answered

Does Experience Ecuador sell personal information?

Experience Ecuador does not describe its operating model as selling personal information. Information may be shared with service providers that support the site or when a user asks to connect with a relevant provider.

What analytics and attribution information is collected?

Analytics may include pages, events, device and browser details, approximate location, referring pages, UTM values, and advertising click identifiers. Attribution may preserve first-touch and recent-touch context for up to 90 days.

Should I send payment details through a contact form?

No. Do not send payment-card numbers or other sensitive financial information through a general inquiry or partner form.

What happens when I follow an external referral?

The external provider becomes responsible for its own site, privacy practices, communications, prices, availability, and booking terms. Review that provider’s policies before submitting information.

How can I request a correction or deletion?

Use the contact page and identify the relevant form, URL, email address, or record. We may request reasonable verification before acting on a request.

Can children submit personal information?

The site is not intended for children under 13 to submit personal information. A parent or guardian should contact us if they believe a child has provided information.

Questions or corrections

Need help with your information?

Use the contact page and include the relevant URL, form, or email address. Do not include sensitive financial information.
