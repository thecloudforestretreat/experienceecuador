The short answer

How should you spend seven days on Ecuador's coast?

Start in Guayaquil, then choose two complementary coastal bases instead of changing hotels every night. A balanced first route pairs the food and nature around Puerto López with either the surf energy of Montañita or a quieter beach stop. Keep weather, marine seasons, road conditions, and your onward flight in the final decision.

Coast itinerary questions

How many days do you need for Ecuador's coast?

Seven days supports Guayaquil and two or three coastal bases. With fewer days, choose one main coastal base and reduce transfers.

Can the Coast and Galapagos be combined?

Yes, especially through Guayaquil, but treat them as separate itinerary blocks and protect enough time for island logistics.

Do marine wildlife experiences operate year-round?

Not always. Wildlife presence, sea conditions, access, and operator schedules vary, so verify the exact activity for your dates.
