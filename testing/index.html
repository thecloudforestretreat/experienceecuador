About Hotel Patio Andaluz

Hotel Patio Andaluz works best for travelers who want atmosphere, location, and historic character rather than a generic city stay. Its restored colonial setting places guests inside Quito's cultural core.

Why this location matters in Quito

The historic center is one of Quito's strongest reasons to stay central. From here, travelers can plan walks around colonial streets, plazas, churches, and museums, then compare broader ideas in the Quito travel guide.

Choosing a colonial hotel in Quito

When comparing colonial hotels in Quito, prioritize walkability, restored architecture, arrival logistics, and access to cultural sites. For other stay options, browse more lodging recommendations.

Things to know before booking

Quito sits at high altitude, so many travelers prefer a slower first day. The historic center is most rewarding during the day, while evening plans are best handled with confirmed local guidance and transportation.
