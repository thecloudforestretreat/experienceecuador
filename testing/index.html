About Hotel Patio Andaluz

Hotel Patio Andaluz is a boutique lodging option for travelers who want to stay close to Quito's heritage core. Its restored colonial setting, classic interior details, and central location make it a practical choice for visitors focused on history, architecture, and walkable sightseeing.

Quito, Andes location context

Quito sits high in the Andes and has one of South America's most recognized historic centers. Staying in this area can reduce transfer time between landmarks, but travelers should also expect narrow streets, active public spaces, and a traditional urban rhythm.

Is this the right stay for your trip?

This hotel is a strong match if you want to wake up inside Quito's cultural district and spend your days walking between churches, plazas, museums, and colonial streets.

It may be less suitable if you are looking for large modern facilities, nightlife-focused neighborhoods, resort-style amenities, or a quieter suburban setting.

Things to know before booking

Quito's altitude can affect some visitors during the first day or two. A centrally located stay can make it easier to explore at a slower pace while adjusting.

The historic center is especially rewarding during the day. For evening plans, travelers should confirm local recommendations with the hotel and use taxis or rides when appropriate.

Staying in Quito's historic center

The old town is the main reason to choose this location. Travelers interested in colonial architecture, public plazas, religious heritage, and city history will usually get more value from staying here than from a purely business-oriented district.
