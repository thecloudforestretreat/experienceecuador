Get discovered by travelers actively planning Ecuador by region and experience. We curate and showcase trusted lodges, hotels, restaurants, guides, tour operators, and activity providers inside our Recommendations experience.

What partners get

Experience Ecuador attracts travelers who are already planning a trip. They are not browsing randomly or scrolling endlessly.

High-intent visibility

  • Placement inside Recommendations and experience pages
  • Filterable by region, location, experience, and category
  • Shown at the moment travelers choose who to contact
  • Direct links to your website and WhatsApp

Clean, trusted presentation

  • Image carousel with clear navigation
  • Short traveler-focused description
  • Optional editorial context explaining why you are recommended
  • Designed for clarity, trust, and comparison

Partner tiers

All partners are reviewed before publication. Visibility level affects placement only. All partners are curated for relevance and quality.

Listed Partner (Free)

  • Included in Recommendations
  • Appears in filters and search
  • Standard placement
  • Free for the first 3 months

Recommended and Featured Partners

  • Higher placement within relevant categories
  • Expanded content and imagery
  • Highlighted or featured positioning
  • Ideal for brands seeking broader exposure

What we need from you

To publish your listing, we request the essentials below. You may submit in English or Spanish.

Assets

  • 3 to 4 square-friendly images
  • Business name and category
  • Primary region and location(s)

Links and contact

  • Website URL
  • WhatsApp or phone number
  • Short description (2 to 5 sentences)
  • Optional menu link or PDF
