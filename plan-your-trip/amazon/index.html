Amazon planning questions

Do I need firm Amazon travel dates?

No. An approximate month or travel window is enough to start. Firm dates are needed before checking lodge, guide and transport availability.

Does submitting this Amazon profile commit me to booking?

No. It starts a no-obligation planning conversation and does not confirm a lodge, service, price or reservation.

Can the profile account for children or mobility needs?

Yes. Include ages, mobility, room layout, dietary needs and comfort expectations so unsuitable access or lodging can be identified early.

Will the profile guarantee wildlife sightings?

No. Wildlife is unpredictable. Ask about habitat, season, guide approach and realistic viewing conditions without expecting a guaranteed species.

Should I choose a lodge before submitting?

Not necessarily. Share the gateway, comfort level, interests and available nights. The team can use those constraints to discuss suitable directions.

What happens after I submit?

The team reviews the profile and may request clarification before discussing recommendations, availability, scope and commercial terms.
