Frequently asked questions

Do I need firm travel dates?

No. An approximate month or two-to-three-week window is enough to begin. Firm dates help when the team checks transport and accommodation feasibility.

Does submitting a profile commit me to booking?

No. The profile is a no-obligation planning tool. It does not confirm a service, reservation, price or itinerary.

Can the profile support a family or larger group?

Yes. Include group size, ages, room preferences, mobility needs, dietary needs and shared interests so the response can account for your group.

Should I submit one profile for several regions?

Start with the region that matters most. Submit another regional profile when a second area has different dates, interests or logistics.

Does this page publish a planning-service fee?

No. Ask the team to explain the planning scope, any applicable fee and what would be delivered before agreeing to paid work.

What happens after I submit?

The team reviews the profile and may follow up with questions, recommendations or next steps. Availability and commercial terms still require confirmation.
