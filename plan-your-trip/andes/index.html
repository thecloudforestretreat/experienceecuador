Planning questions

Do I need firm travel dates?

No. An approximate month or two-to-three-week window is enough to begin. Firm dates help when checking lodge, cruise and flight availability.

Does submitting this profile commit me to booking?

No. The profile is a no-obligation planning tool that helps us understand what would suit your group.

Can you plan for a family or larger group?

Yes. Include your group size, ages, room preferences, mobility needs and shared interests so we can recommend suitable logistics.

What happens after I submit?

Our Ecuador-based team reviews the profile and follows up personally with questions, recommendations or next steps.
